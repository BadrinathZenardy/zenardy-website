Market Outlook Forum LA 2024

by
790 790 people viewed this event.

Zenardy joined the 2024 Market Outlook Forum hosted at LA’s iconic SoFi Stadium – a gathering of top voices in tourism, tech, entertainment, and economic strategy.

Organized by the Los Angeles Tourism & Convention Board, this event spotlighted the future of one of the world’s most dynamic markets. Zenardy’s presence contributed to discussions around economic trends, digital tourism, and AI-driven experience personalization. As a tech company rooted in human impact, our participation emphasized how purpose-driven digital strategies can reshape customer engagement across industries.
