Overview Of Services

Services

NetSuite Services

End-to-end support for NetSuite ERP, including implementation, customization, optimization, and ongoing administration. We help organizations streamline finance, operations, and reporting with tailored NetSuite solutions aligned to business goals.

NetSuite Integrations

We specialize in building robust, real-time integrations between NetSuite and other critical business systems (CRM, eCommerce, payroll, etc.) to eliminate silos, automate processes, and ensure seamless data synchronization.

Celigo Services

Expert services around Celigo’s Integration Platform (iPaaS), enabling businesses to quickly deploy and manage integrations between NetSuite and SaaS/cloud applications with pre-built connectors or custom flows.

Salesforce Services

Connecting Salesforce with NetSuite and other systems to create a unified customer view. We help businesses automate sales operations, improve visibility, and streamline lead-to-cash workflows.

Boomi Services

Leverage Dell Boomi’s powerful integration capabilities to create scalable, low-code workflows that connect cloud and on-premise systems. Ideal for enterprises with complex multi-system landscapes.

EPM Services (NSPB, NSAR, NSAW)

Empowering Financial Planning, Reporting, and Analytics through NetSuite’s EPM Tools NSPB: For smarter forecasting NSAR: For tailored financial reports NSAW: For data-driven insights using unified data models

Automation & Intelligence

Accelerate business performance with intelligent automation and AI solutions. From robotic process automation (RPA) to predictive insights, we enable faster, smarter decisions across departments.

Zen Solutions

Zenardy’s proprietary NetSuite accelerators and toolkits built from real-world project experience. These solutions are designed to reduce implementation time, boost productivity, and solve common ERP challenges with precision.

Latest Success Stories

Success

Robust Financial Transformation for a POS Software Enterprise

We led a full-scale NetSuite implementation for a fast-growing software company specializing in point-of-sale solutions.

Highlights

  • Deployed core modules including Procurement, Order Management, Record to Report, Revenue Management, and Fixed Asset Management.
  • Integrated Salesforce and Ordway with NetSuite for seamless order processing and data flow.
  • Customized multi-level approval workflows for POs and Vendor Bills to ensure audit control and compliance.
  • Delivered solutions to auto-fulfill items for both COGS and leased inventory.
  • Automated Fixed Asset creation for lease-based items to streamline depreciation tracking.
  • Built a fully integrated RMA process between Salesforce and NetSuite to enhance customer service and returns management.

Global Financial Infrastructure for a Multi-Subsidiary Organization

We supported the financial structure for a multinational firm operating across 44 subsidiaries.

Key Achievements

  • Implemented entity-specific charts of accounts and a unified multi-book accounting framework.
  • Designed role-based workflows to streamline AP and AR processes across regions.
  • Automated bulk uploads for payables and receivables to reduce manual entry and improve data accuracy.
  • Integrated a third-party property and lease management system with NetSuite for unified operational oversight.

Intelligent Budgeting and Planning Enablement

We enhanced financial planning capabilities by integrating NetSuite Planning and Budgeting (NSPB) with the core ERP.

Impact

  • Established a four-tier approval structure with saved search-based visibility to ensure accountability at each level.
  • Enabled centralized budget creation, real-time actuals syncing, and role-based forecasting access for departments.
  • Delivered custom dashboards and models to empower advanced forecasting and scenario planning.
  • Streamlined budget vs. actual variance analysis directly within NetSuite for executive review.

End-to-End Automation for Revenue and Expense Management

We drove automation and efficiency for a services-based organization by optimizing AR, commissions, and partner payments.

Solutions Delivered

  • Integrated NetSuite with the client’s admin system for Accounts Receivable automation.
  • Built a custom commission engine using referral-based percentages and tied it into automated AP workflows.
  • Implemented a bulk payment approval system to accelerate disbursements while maintaining control.
  • Supported integration and data mapping with Divvy, BambooHR, HubSpot, and other tools via SuiteApp and custom scripts.
  • Migrated historical trial balance data into NetSuite, ensuring continuity and reporting accuracy.
  • Integrated VersaPay for seamless client collections and embedded payment-enabled invoice templates to enhance the payment experience.
