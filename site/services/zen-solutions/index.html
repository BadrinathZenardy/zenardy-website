Zen Solutions: Pre-Built Accelerators for Fast-Track Transformation

Zen Solutions are a proprietary suite of pre-built accelerators crafted by Zenardy to address real-world business challenges with speed and precision. These customizable modules integrate seamlessly with ERP, CRM, and cloud platforms to enhance functionality in areas like procurement, budgeting, inventory, fulfilment, and reporting. Designed for quick deployment and tailored to your operational needs, Zen Solutions reduce implementation time, and fast-track your digital transformation with enterprise-grade reliability

Zen Budget Management Solution

Challenge: Lack of visibility and control over departmental budgets.

Solution: Deployed Zen Budget Management Solution for real-time planning, allocation, and approvals.

Outcome:

  • 100% digital budget workflows
  • 2x faster approval cycles
  • Role-based access for department-wise control

Zen Auto Fulfillment Solution

Challenge: Delays in order fulfillment and manual shipping updates.

Solution: Implemented automated order-to-shipping workflow integrated with NetSuite.

Outcome:

  • Same-day fulfillment for 85% of orders
  • 90% reduction in manual entries
  • Live tracking and status sync with customers

Zen Advanced Allocation Engine

Challenge: Complex cost allocation rules leading to errors in financial reporting.

Solution: Automated allocation based on department, location, project, and custom dimensions.

Outcome:

  • 100% accurate allocations
  • Reduced monthly close time by 40%
  • Full audit traceability across GL entries

Zen Catch Weight Automation

Challenge: Inconsistent weight-based billing in product fulfillment.

Solution: Deployed catch weight logic integrated into NetSuite item fulfillment and invoicing.

Outcome:

  • Improved billing accuracy
  • 70% reduction in weight-related disputes
  • Seamless integration with scale devices

Zen Mobile Order Printing

Challenge: Manual intervention required to print orders at fulfillment centers.

Solution: Enabled mobile-triggered print commands for pick/pack/ship documents via NetSuite.

Outcome:

  • 3x faster order dispatch
  • Reduced dependency on desktop terminals
  • Real-time document availability on mobile
